1b · Derived output DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS · OVERRIDE KIND-LOCKED

No unit is set here — and instead of deriving nothing from a product of different units, dimensional analysis composes the operands': dims add, factors multiply, so A × s ships as A·s — dimensionally charge. The picker locks to that dimension: the composition itself (prefixable) beside the dimension's named units (Ah, C) — here time is an operand, so there is nothing to choose about it. Derivative (below) of an Ah charge counter over d/ds ships as Ah/s, dimensionally current — switch the function's time unit to h (Ah/h) or pick the named A; the ×3600 falls out of the analysis either way, not a special case.

3 · Plot PER-UNIT LANES · CONVERT TO CONVERGE

Per-unit y mode as the real control lays it out: one stacked lane per distinct unit — its own canvas, its own y-scale, its own slice of the side list, the unit riding the lane's head as [mV]. mV is a different unit than V, so the cell voltage gets a lane of its own. The convert affordance is in the row's readout: the unit beside the value is the chip — click it, pick a like-kind unit, and the series converts and its lane merges into the target unit's lane. Try mV → V.
