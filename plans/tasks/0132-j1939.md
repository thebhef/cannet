# 0132 — J1939

> **Opened 2026-08-31** by owner instruction: *"on J1939, TP decoding
> would be a nice feature, but probably just one of a handful of basic
> functions a complete J1939 implementation should support. DM1
> decoding over TP, BAM, etc. would all be good to have."* No J1939
> task existed to update; this one captures the set.

The basic functions a complete J1939 implementation supports, as one
groomable set:

- **PGN-aware identification and decode** — split the 29-bit id into
  priority / PGN / source address, match PDU1 messages on the PGN
  rather than the whole id, and show PGN / SA / DA wherever J1939
  traffic is on screen.
- **Transport Protocol reassembly** — TP.CM / TP.DT in both shapes,
  RTS/CTS (connection mode) and **BAM** (broadcast) — reassembled into
  the carried PGN and decoded against the database like any other
  message.
- **Diagnostics** — **DM1** active DTCs (lamp status plus SPN / FMI /
  occurrence-count lists), including the more-than-8-byte case that
  arrives over TP; DM2 previously-active as the natural sibling.
- **Address claim** (PGN 60928) — track which NAME sits at which
  source address, so a trace can say who is talking.

## Open questions

- **A shared protocol concept** (suggestion relayed by the owner,
  2026-09-02): serve J1939 and CANopen (task 22) through one host-side
  protocol-decoder abstraction — a decoder per bus that consumes the
  frame stream and emits reassembled messages into the trace model,
  frame annotations, and a protocol status surface — rather than two
  unrelated custom modules, so a third protocol pays only for its own
  logic, not another round of model/UI plumbing. How far "spec instead
  of code" can go within that is part of the question: id carving and
  payload schemas are naturally data-driven, while the transport state
  machines (TP's BAM/RTS-CTS vs SDO's request/response) may resist a
  common spec language. To be settled when the first of the two tasks
  is groomed; whichever grooms first designs the concept.

- Where reassembly lives (the host model, per `CLAUDE.md`) and how a
  reassembled message appears in the trace beside its constituent
  frames.
- Whether J1939 handling is per bus, per database, or inferred from
  the traffic.
- Transmit-side scope — requests (PGN 59904), TP transmit, RBS over
  J1939 — in or out for the first pass.
- SPN naming: the assigned DBC only, or a built-in SPN table.

## Exit criteria

Groomed when scheduled; at minimum: a captured BAM and an RTS/CTS
session each reassemble and decode against the database; a
more-than-8-byte DM1 renders its DTC list; the behaviours are pinned
by tests over fixture captures.
